# 🍌 Banana Cookies

## Ingredients

- 190 g all-purpose flour
- 230 g ripe bananas, peeled (about 2 medium bananas)
- 100 g granulated sugar
- 57 g unsalted butter
- 50 g egg (about 1 large egg, without shell)
- 5 g baking soda
- 1.5 g salt

## Instructions

> The original image only provides the ingredients. The method below is a standard method suitable for this type of cookie.

1. Preheat the oven to **180°C / 350°F**.
2. Mash the bananas with a fork until smooth.
3. Soften the butter and mix it with the sugar.
4. Add the egg and mix until combined.
5. Stir in the mashed bananas.
6. In a separate bowl, combine the flour, baking soda, and salt.
7. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the banana mixture. Mix just until combined; avoid overmixing.
8. Drop portions of dough onto a baking sheet lined with parchment paper, leaving some space between each cookie.
9. Bake for approximately **10–14 minutes at 180°C / 350°F**, until the edges are lightly golden.
10. Leave the cookies on the baking sheet for a few minutes before transferring them to a wire rack.

These cookies will have a **soft, cake-like texture** because of the relatively high amount of banana.
