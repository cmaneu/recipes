# Sugar-free banana bread

![Sugar-free banana bread](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cd31f376-cdb9-4630-b957-1ff97056b689)

## Ingredients

- 5 bananas
- 1/2 sachet of baking powder
- 150 g of wheat flour
- 100 g of softened butter
- 2 eggs
- 80 g of hazelnut powder
- 60 ml of milk
- 50 g of chocolate chips
- 1 teaspoon of four-spice mix

## Preparation

1. Mix the flour, baking powder, hazelnut powder, chocolate chips and four-spice mix.
2. Add the butter, milk, eggs and 4 mashed bananas.
3. Pour into a buttered loaf tin.
4. Slice the last banana lengthwise, then place the 2 pieces on top of the cake batter.
5. Bake for 30 minutes at 180°C.
