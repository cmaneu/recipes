# Sugar-free banana bread

![Sugar-free banana bread](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cd31f376-cdb9-4630-b957-1ff97056b689)

## Ingredients

- 3 very ripe bananas
- 2 eggs
- 200g of flour
- 1/2 sachet of baking powder
- 100g of unsweetened apple sauce
- 60g of melted butter
- 1 pinch of salt
- (optional) 60g of dark chocolate chips

## Preparation

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
2. Mash the bananas in a large bowl.
3. Add the eggs, apple sauce and melted butter, then mix.
4. Add the flour, baking powder and salt. Fold in the chocolate chips if using.
5. Pour the batter into a buttered (or lined) loaf tin and bake for 40 to 45 minutes.
6. Check doneness with the tip of a knife, then let it cool slightly before unmoulding.

Source: https://www.marmiton.org/recettes/recette_banana-bread-sans-sucre_391903.aspx (baking powder reduced to 1/2 sachet)
