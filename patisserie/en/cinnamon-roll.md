# Cinnamon rolls

Makes 12 rolls.

Preparation: 30 minutes · Rising: about 50 minutes · Baking: 25–30 minutes.

## Ingredients

### Dough

- 240 ml milk, warmed to about 46°C
- 8 g instant dry yeast
- 2 large eggs, at room temperature
- 75 g salted butter, very soft or melted and cooled below 43°C
- 100 g granulated sugar
- 6 g fine salt
- 500 g bread flour, plus up to 60 g more if needed and a little for dusting
- A little butter or oil for greasing the bowl and baking dish

### Cinnamon filling

- 115 g salted butter, very soft
- 215 g soft brown sugar
- 16 g ground cinnamon

### Before baking

- 120 ml heavy cream, about 35% fat

### Cream cheese frosting

- 170 g cream cheese, softened
- 75 g salted butter, softened
- 240 g icing sugar
- 7.5 ml maple or vanilla extract

## Preparation

1. Stir the yeast into the warm milk in the bowl of a stand mixer. Leave for 3–4 minutes, until a little foam appears.
2. Mix in the eggs, 75 g butter and granulated sugar. Add the salt and 500 g flour, then mix with the paddle attachment until no loose flour remains. Leave the dough to rest for 5 minutes.
3. Switch to the dough hook and knead at medium speed for 5–7 minutes. If the dough is too wet to come together, add some of the reserved 60 g flour gradually. Stop adding flour once the dough is smooth, elastic and slightly sticky; it should release from the sides of the bowl but may still cling to the bottom.
4. Transfer to a lightly greased bowl and cover with a clean towel. Leave somewhere warm until doubled in volume, about 30 minutes depending on the room temperature. Move on once it has doubled rather than letting it continue to rise.
5. Meanwhile, make the filling by mixing the 115 g soft butter with the brown sugar and cinnamon.
6. On a floured worktop, roll the dough into a rectangle about 61 × 38 cm. Spread the filling evenly over it, then roll up snugly from one of the long edges.
7. Divide the log into 12 equal pieces and arrange in a greased 23 × 33 cm baking dish. Cover and leave to rise for about 20 minutes. Meanwhile, preheat the oven to 190°C with conventional heat.
8. Warm the cream gently until lukewarm, not hot. After the second rise, pour it over the rolls so it runs between them.
9. Bake for 25–30 minutes, until lightly golden and cooked through in the middle. Check after 20 minutes; if the tops are browning too quickly, cover loosely with foil for the rest of the baking time.
10. Leave the rolls to cool. Beat the cream cheese with the remaining 75 g butter until smooth, then beat in the icing sugar and extract. Spread over the cooled rolls.

## Notes

- Metric quantities are rounded conversions. Flour weight varies with how it is measured, so use the dough's texture as a guide rather than adding all the extra flour automatically.
- All-purpose flour can replace bread flour, although the rolls will have a slightly different texture.
