# Double chocolate Swiss roll

Serves 8

- Preparation: 20 minutes
- Baking: 9 to 11 minutes
- Chilling: at least 2 hours

## Ingredients

### For the chocolate sponge

- 4 large eggs, at room temperature
- 125 g granulated sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 45 ml sunflower oil
- 71 g all-purpose flour
- 21 g unsweetened cocoa powder, plus extra for dusting
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder

### For the chocolate mousse

- 260 ml cold heavy cream, ideally 35% fat
- 85 g dark or baking chocolate with 50-55% cocoa, finely chopped

## Preparation

### Make the sponge

1. Preheat the oven to 175°C conventional. Lightly grease a rimmed baking sheet measuring about 43 x 28 cm (or 38 x 25 cm), then line it with parchment paper.
1. Beat the eggs at medium-high speed for 3 minutes. Gradually add the sugar, then continue beating for 4 to 5 minutes. The mixture should nearly triple in volume, become pale and thick, and form a ribbon that remains visible on the surface for a few seconds.
1. Mix in the vanilla and salt. Slowly pour in the sunflower oil while mixing gently.
1. Sift the flour, cocoa powder and baking powder together, then sift the mixture again over the eggs. Gently fold it in with a spatula in two additions, taking care not to deflate the batter.
1. Pour the batter onto the baking sheet and spread it into a thin, even layer. Bake for 9 to 11 minutes. The sponge is ready when it springs back after being lightly pressed. Do not overbake it, or it may dry out and crack when rolled.

### Roll the sponge

1. Let the sponge cool for about 1 minute, then lightly dust the surface with cocoa powder.
1. Loosen the edges with a knife and turn the sponge out onto a clean tea towel or a fresh sheet of parchment paper. Carefully peel off the parchment used for baking.
1. Immediately roll up the warm sponge with the tea towel inside. Leave it rolled until completely cool.

### Make the chocolate mousse

1. Heat 80 ml of the cream until it just begins to simmer. Remove it from the heat, add the chocolate and wait 1 minute before stirring until the ganache is smooth and glossy. Let it cool for about 5 minutes.
1. Whip the remaining 180 ml of cream to stiff but still supple peaks, about 2 to 4 minutes with an electric mixer. Stop as soon as the peaks hold and the whisk marks remain visible.
1. Gently fold the cooled ganache into the whipped cream with a spatula.

### Assemble the roll

1. Carefully unroll the cooled sponge.
1. Spread the chocolate mousse evenly over it, leaving a margin of about 1 cm around the edges. Add a little more filling along the edge that will form the centre of the spiral.
1. Gently roll the cake up again, then place it seam-side down on a serving plate.
1. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours. Lightly dust with cocoa powder before serving.

## Tips

- Room-temperature eggs produce the greatest volume.
- Do not replace the oil with butter: oil keeps the sponge flexible after chilling.
- Roll the sponge while it is still warm and avoid overbaking it to prevent cracks.
- Use very cold heavy cream. Cream with 30% fat can work, but the mousse will be slightly softer.
- Do not overwhip the cream: it will become grainy and eventually turn into butter.
